Fees, homework, and how it works

Each grind lasts one hour and costs €25 per student per session. There is a maximum of four students in each class so everyone can participate and receive attention.

Every student is followed individually. Progress is tracked over the year with graphs that reflect improvement alongside test results and class performance, so strengths and next steps stay visible.

Lessons combine structure and immersion: we cover grammar systematically, and we also use debates, homework, recommended films and series, and music so English comes from real situations, not only a textbook.
